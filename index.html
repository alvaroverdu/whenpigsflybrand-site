Why envelopes work

Money you can see is money you can plan.

You see it fill up

A balance in an app is just a number. A stack of full envelopes is progress you can hold, and that's what keeps you going on week six.

Small amounts count

Some weeks you stuff $3, some weeks $80. Every envelope moves the total, so a tight month doesn't break the habit.

You decide first

The money for a goal goes in before it gets spent on something else. That's the whole trick, and it works on any income.

Questions

Before you start

How much do I save with the 100 envelopes?

Envelope 1 gets $1, envelope 2 gets $2, all the way to $100. All 100 add up to $5,050. You choose the order and the pace: some people do one a day, others one a week.

Do the envelopes fit real bills?

Yes. They are sized for US dollar bills, and the binder still closes when they are full.

Can I reuse the challenges?

Yes. They are laminated: cross numbers off with the dry-erase marker and wipe them clean when you're done.

Where do I buy it?

On our TikTok Shop and on Amazon. Shipping, returns and payment are handled by the store you choose.

How do I print the free challenges?

Print at 100% scale (not "fit to page") so the holes line up with the binder rings, then cut, punch and color in as you save.

Who is When Pigs Fly?

A small US brand of envelope budgeting binders and savings challenges, for people who'd rather see their money than guess where it went.
