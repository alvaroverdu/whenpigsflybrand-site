Privacy Policy

This site, whenpigsflybrand.com, is run by MULTISIA LLC, a New Mexico limited liability company that sells When Pigs Fly products in the United States ("we", "us"). This page explains what we collect here, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Your email address, only if you type it into the free challenges form.
  • Anonymous visit statistics: pages viewed, the site that sent you, country and device type, counted in total. We use a cookie-free analytics tool, so we don't set cookies or follow you around other sites.
  • Anything you write to us at contact@whenpigsflybrand.com.

We don't sell anything on this site and we never see your payment details. Purchases happen on TikTok Shop or Amazon, under their own privacy policies.

Why we use it

  • To email you the printable challenges you asked for, and then occasional emails with new challenges, budgeting ideas and news about our products. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • To answer your messages.
  • To understand, in total, which pages are useful so we can improve the site.

Who else handles it

Your email address is stored by the email service we use to send our emails, which acts on our behalf and can't use it for anything else. Our site is hosted by a web hosting provider that processes standard technical data (like IP addresses) to deliver the pages. We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising, and we never will.

Our team and the services we use may store or access your information outside the United States, including in the European Union.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We keep emails you send us for as long as we need them to help you, and then delete them.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe at any time from the link in any email. Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it: write to contact@whenpigsflybrand.com and we'll answer within 30 days. Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws have these rights by law; we give them to everyone. We will never treat you differently for using them.

Children

This site is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed up, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page. If the change is important, we'll tell subscribers by email.
